Privacy Policy

Your routines stay with you.

Effective October 11, 2026 · Developer: Will Li

This policy describes I Did It (记好), the iPhone and Apple Watch app, and this support website.

Data in the app

The app stores routine names, notes, optional photos, schedules, preferences, and completion history on your devices. The developer does not operate an app-data server or receive these records automatically. The app has no account system, advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDK.

Your paired iPhone and Watch exchange app data using Apple's WatchConnectivity service. System device backups may include app data under your device settings. These are separate from a developer-run cloud service.

Permissions you control

  • Microphone and speech: Recording begins only when you start Voice Log. Recognition is requested on-device. Audio is not saved by the app; leaving the screen stops recording. You review recognized text before saving. Keyboard dictation and Siri are separate Apple features controlled by system settings.
  • Photos and camera: The photo you choose or take is saved with the routine and may be synced to your paired Watch or included in an exported backup.
  • Apple Health: On supported versions, the app reads only the medication names you authorize. Selected names are stored in routine notes when you save. It does not write completion records to Health or use Health data for advertising.
  • Notifications and speech output: Local notifications can display routine names. Read Aloud speaks routine details through the system audio output. People nearby may see or hear them.

You can change permissions in system settings and continue using manual entry where applicable.

Backups, retention, and deletion

Records remain until you delete them. Exported backups include routines, photos, and history and are not encrypted by the app. You choose where to save or share them. Restoring a backup replaces app data and keeps a local recovery backup first.

Erase All Data removes the app's routines and records, then syncs the deletion to the paired Watch when connected. Exported files, independently saved recovery files, and system device backups require separate management in their storage locations.

If you contact support

Email sent to weilifilm@gmail.com includes your address and whatever content you choose to provide. It is used to respond to your request and is handled by email service providers. Do not send sensitive routine details or photos unless necessary. You may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting the same address.

This website

This static support site does not add analytics, advertising, forms, or tracking cookies. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which logs visitors' IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for the host's practices.

Changes and questions

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version. Contact Will Li at weilifilm@gmail.com with privacy questions.
